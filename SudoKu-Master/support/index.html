Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. SudoKu Master has no account and no login. You open the app and play.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Every puzzle, lesson and record is on your device, and you can play offline. A connection is only used for the App Store (buying or restoring the subscription) and, from version 1.1.0, for the optional ads. If an ad cannot load because you are offline, the hint or Auto Notes use it would have paid for is free.

Why are some difficulties and levels locked?

Beginner, Easy and Medium are open from the start. Hard, Expert and Nightmare each unlock after you complete 5 levels in the difficulty below. Within a difficulty, levels unlock two ahead of your last completed level, so finishing the level you are on opens the next one.

How does the Learn section work?

Learn gives you one new technique lesson per day. Stage 1 is open from the start, and later stages unlock as you complete lessons.

Can I practise a technique more than once?

Yes. Practice level 1 of every technique is free and can be replayed as often as you like.

What is Adventure?

Adventure gives you two new shape puzzles per day. Replaying a puzzle you have already opened is always free.

What do hints actually tell me?

A hint names the technique that applies to the current board, so you learn the method instead of just being handed a number.

Is there a limit on hints and Auto Notes?

From version 1.1.0, free players get 3 hints and 2 uses of Auto Notes a day. After that you can choose to watch a short ad for one more each time, or subscribe to SudoKu Master Pro for unlimited use. Before 1.1.0 there is no limit.

Are there ads?

From version 1.1.0, only if you choose to watch one. A short rewarded ad from Google AdMob can add one more hint, one more Auto Notes use, or the next practice level. Nothing plays unless you tap the button that says it will; there are no banner ads and no ads between games. Ads are non-personalised, the app never asks to track you, and subscribers never see an ad offer.

Can I see how a puzzle was solved?

Yes. Every finished puzzle keeps a full move replay you can step through.

Is there a subscription?

Yes, from version 1.1.0: SudoKu Master Pro, monthly or yearly (US$3.99 a month or US$29.99 a year in the United States; the app shows the price for your country). Both plans unlock the same things: every lesson and all 50 practice levels per technique, jumping into a puzzle at the step where a technique applies, every difficulty and level, unlimited hints, Auto Notes and Adventure puzzles, Auto Fill, and no ads. The free app stays a complete game. Open it from Settings → SudoKu Master Pro. The subscription is sold through Apple's App Store.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open SudoKu Master's Settings → Manage Subscription, or the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → SudoKu Master Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. We cannot cancel or refund a subscription for you: Apple handles billing (refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com).

I subscribed but Pro is not unlocked, or I have a new device

Open Settings → SudoKu Master Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with. Restoring brings back the subscription only; puzzle progress stays on the device it was made on (see below).

Will my progress survive if I delete the app?

No. Progress is stored on your device only. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress, and it cannot be restored or moved to another device.

Report a bug

Email kysh@1609007bc.ca with as much of the following as you can:

  • The steps that led to the problem, in order, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot of the screen where it went wrong.
  • The puzzle's difficulty and level number.
  • Your app version, device and iOS version.

A screenshot of the board is usually enough for us to reproduce a puzzle bug exactly.

Privacy

SudoKu Master itself collects no data and we run no server. From version 1.1.0, Google's ad software for the optional ads receives device information as described in the policy. The full policy is at SudoKu Master Privacy Policy.
